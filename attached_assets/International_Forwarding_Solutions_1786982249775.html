Ocean freight is complex, high-volume, and built on long lead times and tight margins. CargoWise gives you full visibility across bookings, schedules, and container movements, with direct carrier connections and workflows built to handle every stage from port to port. Plan routes, manage capacity, and monitor shipments in one system designed to keep global freight moving with structure and certainty.

Ocean solutions

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Overland reach.
Optimized handoffs.

Landside logistics is fragmented, time-sensitive, and often invisible once containers leave the port. CargoWise drives your inland operations from first mile to last, whether it’s port drayage, container pickup and delivery, or long-haul rail. Movements across carriers, terminals, and legs are assigned, tracked, and managed in a single workflow, keeping landside logistics fully aligned with the rest of your forwarding operation.

Workflows

Enterprise-grade control for complex global operations

  1. Trigger the right task at the right time.

    Configure workflow templates by mode, lane, direction, or customer to speed up data entry, standardize execution, and ensure shipments follow the right process from the start.

  2. Track accountability at every step.

    With clear task assignments and real-time job visibility, your teams know exactly what’s due, who owns it, and what comes next.

  3. Automate communication and exception handling.

    Send documents, trigger milestone alerts, and notify parties when actions are due or complete, with configurable alerts and exception logs that help you respond quickly, reduce risk, and prevent repeat errors.
