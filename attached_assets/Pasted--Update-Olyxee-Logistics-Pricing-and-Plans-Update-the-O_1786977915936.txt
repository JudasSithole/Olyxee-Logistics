# Update Olyxee Logistics Pricing and Plans

Update the Olyxee Logistics pricing, subscription logic, and plan presentation.

## Free Plan — R0/month

Keep the current Free plan available with:

* Customer management
* Order management
* Invoicing
* Payment confirmation
* Air and sea shipment workflows
* Shipment tracking
* Branded customer tracking pages
* Delivery and collection management
* Maximum of 50 automated email notifications per company per month

Remove all SMS functionality from the product and pricing pages for now.

Existing companies should remain on the Free plan without requiring payment.

## Scale Plan — R1,499/month

Create a new paid plan called **Scale**.

Scale should include everything in Free, with higher usage limits and access to the advanced capabilities that are introduced over time, including Orgni Intelligence.

Position Scale around helping freight companies reduce repetitive operational work as they grow.

Do not position the upgrade primarily around more emails. Position it around operational scale, automation, and intelligence.

Scale customers should eventually receive:

* Orgni Intelligence
* Higher/unlimited email allowance based on fair-use limits
* Automated follow-ups
* Document monitoring
* Exception detection
* ETA and deadline monitoring
* Operational alerts
* Easier customs-clearance workflows
* Task escalation
* Customer communication automation
* Call-center capabilities when available
* Future intelligent operational automation

Clearly mark capabilities that are not yet released as **Coming Soon** rather than presenting them as currently available.

## Billing Start Date

Do not charge anyone for Scale immediately.

Allow businesses to use the platform during the current rollout period.

**Scale billing should begin on September 30, 2026.**

Before that date:

* Users can see the Scale plan and its price.
* Eligible companies can select or join Scale.
* Clearly communicate that billing begins September 30, 2026.
* Do not process a subscription charge before that date.

After billing begins, Scale should cost **R1,499 per company per month**.

## Product Messaging

Use simple business language.

Free:

**Run your freight operation in one place.**

Scale:

**Grow your freight operation with less manual work. Let Orgni help your team monitor jobs, handle routine follow-ups, and surface what actually needs attention.**

Avoid technical AI jargon.

The main value proposition for Scale should be:

**Handle more shipments without increasing repetitive admin at the same rate.**

## Important

* Completely remove SMS as a current feature.
* Do not break existing customer, order, invoice, shipment, tracking, email, or notification functionality.
* Preserve all current Free-plan user data.
* Do not gate existing Free functionality behind Scale.
* Update pricing cards, account/subscription screens, plan labels, usage limits, and backend entitlement logic consistently.
* Use September 30, 2026 as the Scale billing start date everywhere it is displayed.
