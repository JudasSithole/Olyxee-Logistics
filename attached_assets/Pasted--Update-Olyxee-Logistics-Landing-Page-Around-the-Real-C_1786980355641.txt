# Update Olyxee Logistics Landing Page Around the Real Current Workflow

Update `logistics.olyxee.com` while preserving the existing visual style, layout language, typography, colors, spacing, animations, and premium/minimal feel.

Do not redesign the page from scratch.

The landing page should explain Olyxee Logistics by showing the **real workflow that exists today**, step by step.

Do not mix future Orgni capabilities into the current Free product.

## Target Customer

Speak directly to:

* Freight forwarders
* Clearing & forwarding companies
* Import/export businesses
* Cross-border freight operators
* Air and sea freight businesses

Especially small and mid-sized operators handling routes such as China → South Africa and other international/cross-border freight.

## Show the Current Workflow

Make this the main story of the page.

### 1. Add your customer

Keep the customer and their freight activity connected in one place.

Visual:

**Customer**

↓

### 2. Create and send a quote

Create a quote for the shipment.

The customer can accept or reject it.

When accepted, convert it into an order without starting again.

Visual:

**Customer → Quote → Accepted → Order**

Value:

**Move from sales to operations without recreating the job.**

### 3. Add the freight details

Add the shipment information your team needs.

Examples:

* Air or sea freight
* Origin
* Destination
* Cargo information
* Supplier tracking/reference
* Warehouse reference when available

Visual:

**Order → Freight Details**

Value:

**Keep the important shipment information attached to the same order.**

### 4. Move the shipment through its stages

Your team updates the shipment as it progresses.

For air freight, show an example such as:

**Order Confirmed**
→ **Received from Supplier**
→ **Export Customs Cleared**
→ **In Transit**
→ **Import Customs Clearance**
→ **Out for Delivery**
→ **Delivered**

For sea freight, show the relevant sea/vessel/port stages already supported by the product.

Do not imply these updates happen automatically today.

Make it clear that the operations team manages the progress from Olyxee.

### 5. Keep the customer informed

As the order progresses, customers can follow the shipment through a public branded tracking page.

Show:

**Shipment Status → Customer Tracking Page**

The customer should not need an Olyxee account to track their shipment.

Mention automated email updates only where currently supported.

Free companies receive up to **50 automated emails per month**.

Do not mention SMS.

### 6. Keep invoice and payment status connected

Allow the team to keep basic invoice and payment information connected to the order.

Use simple language:

**Know which freight jobs are paid and which are still outstanding.**

Do not present advanced accounting or automated payment reconciliation as available.

### 7. Keep the job history

Keep the shipment status history, documents, customer information and completed order record together.

Value:

**You can go back and see what happened without rebuilding the history from messages and spreadsheets.**
